# Recover an I96 8K

Undo everything:

```bash
./undo.sh <box ip>
```

If the box does not return after a reboot, unplug its power for 10 seconds and plug it back in. This happened
once in about 20 reboots during testing. The cause was not found, and no data was lost.

See [docs/RECOVERY_GUIDE.md](../../docs/RECOVERY_GUIDE.md) for every other case.
