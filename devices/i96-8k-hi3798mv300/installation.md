# Install on an I96 8K

1. Connect the box to Wi-Fi and note its IP address from Settings > Network & Internet.
2. Preview the changes, then install:

```bash
./install.sh --dry-run <box ip>
./install.sh <box ip>
```

The installer asks whether to apply a logo, then finishes with a health check. Afterwards, sign in to your apps.
The first press of the voice button can show a Google permission prompt, and the first Chrome launch shows its
welcome screen.
